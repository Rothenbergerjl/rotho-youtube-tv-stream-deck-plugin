Slots are given to YouTube TV, Peacock and Paramount+ player tabs in the order they open. Slot 1 is the first game you opened. The badge in the bottom-left corner shows the service. With Team colors on, a game between known teams shows each team's colors; turn it off for plain text.

With Long press on Focus, holding the key for half a second also brings the game's tab and window to the front.

With Show score on, a game between two known teams shows the live score from ESPN. The stream runs behind the live game, so Score delay holds each new score back to avoid spoilers; raise it if scores still change before you see the play. Score alerts flash the scoring team's row and the points yellow, then keep the points in the middle of the key.
